### Extended description (for the entry body, if the page supports one)

*Corridor Dynamics* is the integration paper for the CIRIS research series. It
makes one empirical claim and one bet, held separately.

The empirical claim: coordinated systems occupy a bounded corridor between
rigidity (single-voice collapse) and chaos (dispersal), and non-corridor systems
either dissolve quickly or persist only under documented active maintenance. The
paper anchors this with paired in-corridor / out-corridor data across five
substrates — *C. elegans* and *Drosophila* neural activity, four LLM
architectures, four major open-source projects, healthy versus tumor tissue
across five cancers, and centuries-persisting social groups against cults and
dissolved movements. The would-be falsifier — long unmaintained persistence
outside the corridor — is absent across all five substrates.

The bet: that this structural object recurs at every scale where coordination
happens, and that engineering aligned AI is engineering corridor occupation at
the conscience layer. The universal-scale tier of the bet is held openly as a
research program, not as established science. The engineering tier — CCA v3,
RATCHET, CRC v1, and the CIRISAgent framework — stands at its own DOIs
regardless.

This paper cites the prior three papers in the series as its empirical anchor;
it does not re-present them.
